# Design document template

Copy the four sections below into a new chapter. Give it a title that names the change.

## Summary

What should reviewers decide? Once the choice is settled, state the outcome here.

## Motivation

Describe a case that led to the change. What happens today, and why does someone care?

## Design

Name the decisions being added, revised, or removed. Give the main sentence of each affected anchor and link to its full entry. For a removal, say which rule is being retired and why. One document can cover several related decisions and anchors.

## FAQ

What other approach did you seriously consider? Why did you choose this one? Record the questions whose answers a future reader would otherwise have to reconstruct.
